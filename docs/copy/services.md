## How we work — light, second half of page

Eyebrow: `HOW WE WORK`
H2: `You see the work before you pay for it`

Five-step process (numbered, from company profile p.6, kept intact — it is a
differentiator competitors don't publish):
1. **A short scoping call** — Thirty minutes. We establish what you are actually
   required to fix, by when, and who is asking. No charge, no obligation to continue.
2. **Something concrete, free** — Before you commit, you get a real deliverable: a
   findings sheet on your current exposure, or a working mock-up of the site. Built from
   your situation, not a template.
3. **A fixed price** — Scope and price agreed in writing before work starts. We do not
   bill by the hour, and we do not revise the number mid-engagement unless you change
   what you asked for.
4. **Delivery, then handover** — You get the documentation, the credentials and the
   administrative access. Everything we build is yours, and you are never locked in by
   us holding a password.
5. **The retainer, if it earns its place** — Ongoing work is a separate decision, taken
   after delivery. Some clients need it monthly, some annually, some not at all.

### How we quote — three shapes

- **A one-off project** — A website, a tenant hardening, a firewall build. Fixed price
  agreed up front and billed against milestones. Nothing recurring unless you ask for it.
- **A programme with a retainer** — Security awareness works this way. The build is
  one-off; running the campaigns and producing the reports is monthly or annual, and you
  decide which after the build.
- **An annual retainer** — Hosting, domains, email and support, billed once a year
  against one invoice and one purchase order.

Terms line: `Website work carries a 50% deposit. Everything else is agreed at quotation. We do not bill by the hour.`
