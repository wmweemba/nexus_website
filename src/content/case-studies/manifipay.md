Public website for a Lusaka lender, wired directly into their loan system so an online
enquiry becomes a reviewable application without anyone re-typing it. An enquiry
submitted on the public site lands directly in ManifiPay's own review queue — no
re-keying, no gap between a visitor's interest and a reviewable application.
